Latest EPF updatesUpdated 7 Sept 2026
  • NewJoint Declaration corrections are filed online through the member portal. Name changes need your employer to countersign.
  • ImportantAround one in four EPF claims was rejected or returned last year. Most failures are a single field that does not match.
  • Aadhaar seeding is required before an online claim can be settled. The name must match character for character.
  • EPFO never asks for your OTP, password or bank details over a call. Claimity will never ask for them either.
  • A rejected claim is closed and must be filed again. A returned claim stays open and only needs the flagged field corrected.
  • Date of exit is marked by your employer, not by you. A final settlement cannot be processed while it is blank.
  • EPFO works to twenty days on a complete claim. A claim held for verification commonly takes thirty to sixty.
